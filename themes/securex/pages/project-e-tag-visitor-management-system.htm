Smart RFID & Visitor Logging Project

Managing authorized entry and temporary guest access for high-density residential societies and corporate offices requires a unified digital solution.

Technicist engineered and installed an end-to-end RFID E-Tag barrier system combined with digital visitor management software, replacing manual registers with automated check-in and instantaneous host notifications.

Hands-Free Vehicle E-Tag Entry

Long-range UHF readers automatically verify windshield tags and open heavy-duty barrier arms.

QR Code Guest Pre-Registration

Residents send digital entry passes to guests, enabling 5-second check-in at security gates.

CNIC Scan & Photo Verification

Automated OCR scanners capture guest identification and store encrypted timestamps in the central database.
